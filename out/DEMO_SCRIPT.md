# 2-minute demo click path

1. **Map (0:00-0:15)** - "Every dot is a Metro station; the bigger and redder it is, the more riders switch to a bus there, about 55,000 a day across the city."
2. **Play the time bar (0:15-0:35)** - "Press play and watch the morning: trains empty, a wave of riders walks out, and the buses near the station fill and drain behind it. That wave is the pulse, the bus crowd is the echo."
3. **Click Guindy (0:35-0:50)** - "Guindy sends about 3,900 riders a day to buses, 44% of its exits, with an average wait we can read straight off this panel."
4. **Where people go (0:50-1:05)** - "Open Where people go: the biggest stop is Guindy TVK Estate at 17.5% of exits, and the lines show which routes they take."
5. **Regulars (1:05-1:20)** - "These are cards that exit at the same time on most days, the commuters; we can say when they leave and where they likely head."
6. **Fix It (1:20-1:40)** - "Fix It ranks 15 changes: 9 add buses, 3 retime, 3 bigger buses, using 15 extra buses to save about 5,800 passenger-minutes a day."
7. **Timetable tool (1:40-1:52)** - "Slide a route's departures a few minutes and the wait curve updates against real train arrivals, so the best shift is visible."
8. **How it works (1:52-2:00)** - "We group exits into pulses, measure the extra boardings after each, subtract a control-stop placebo (179k down to 55k a day), and these are quick estimates."
